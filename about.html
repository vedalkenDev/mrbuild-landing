Meet Mr Build

The man in the yellow hard hat is our building buddy. He stands for the people at the counter: they know the stock, they know the trade, and they will tell you straight what you need and what you don't.

We sell retail and wholesale from the same counter, so a homeowner fixing a tap and a contractor buying a truckload of cement get the same help.
